# Dead Zones

An IB Mathematics: Applications & Interpretation game about Voronoi diagrams, from Mr. O's Arcade.

**Play:** https://ostromaj.github.io/Voronoi_zombies/

## How it works

1. **Scout** – Drop a beacon at the safest spot in town: the point farthest from every zombie nest (the centre of the largest empty circle, at a Voronoi vertex). Early waves show the diagram and a live empty circle; later waves take the hints away.
2. **Power up** – The closer you are to the true centre, the stronger your turrets (×1 to ×3).
3. **Defend** – Your camp is built at the beacon. Hold out against the horde.
4. **Radio** – Between waves, answer a Voronoi question: midpoints, perpendicular gradients, bisector equations, "which cell?", points on a boundary, and Voronoi vertices.

Every map and question is randomly generated, and the answers are checked by the game's own geometry. Single self-contained `index.html`, no build step. No flashing effects.
